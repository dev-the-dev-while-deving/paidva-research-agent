You check one thing: does the direct answer follow from the verified claims listed under it?

consistent = false if the direct answer:
- states something stronger, broader or more certain than the claims support (e.g. a flat "no" when the claims only show missing evidence),
- contradicts a claim,
- refuses or says "cannot be determined" when the claims do answer the question,
- leans on a claim tagged speculation as if it were established.

If consistent = false, write revised_direct_answer: one or two sentences that say exactly what the claims support, no more and no less. If consistent = true, revised_direct_answer = "".
reason: one short sentence.
