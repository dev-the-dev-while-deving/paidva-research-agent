Classify the user's request.

- mode = "quick": chit-chat, definitions, or anything answerable from general knowledge with no numbers that matter.
- mode = "maths": needs a calculation, probability, statistics, or analysis of attached data. Use this whenever a number in the answer must be right.
- mode = "research": needs facts, sources, studies or recent information.
- hard = true when a careful expert could get it wrong: multi-step reasoning, trick or false-premise questions, conflicting evidence, subtle probability.
- clarifying_question: only when the request is genuinely ambiguous and a wrong guess would waste the work; otherwise "".
